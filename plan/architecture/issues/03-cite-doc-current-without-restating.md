## Answer

The cite question on [How this wiki cites doc/current without restating](plan/architecture/issues/03-cite-doc-current-without-restating.md) is dissolved. There is no outside architecture wiki that cites [[doc/current/]]. This wiki holds Is and Should Become for how it works. It does not restate how to use. It does not restate a Committed Decision. Plans point at the wiki. This answer does not add a gloss-versus-pointer rule. The boundary with how to use and with Committed Decisions is [Boundary vs End-user wiki and Committed Decisions](plan/architecture/issues/04-boundary-vs-end-user-wiki-and-decisions.md).
