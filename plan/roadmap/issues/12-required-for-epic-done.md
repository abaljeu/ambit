# Required for Epic done vs Chapter, no overlap

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 11

## Question

How does focusing on an Epic remind you of related Projects, and when is the Epic done? Some classified Projects belong on a Chapter. Wiki work was classified as supporting every Epic.

## Comments

- 2026-08-30: Split, no overlap. Chapter checklists hold items that belong to a beat. **Required for done** holds the rest. Epic done = every Chapter item plus every Required item (or its named part) done. Parts of a Project may be specified later; until then the line is the whole Project or the remainder after Chapter-owned parts.
- 2026-08-30: An Epic is not done until the wiki portions about that Epic are done. Not the whole wiki Project. File issues under the wiki Projects; point at those issues from the Epic. Unfiled portions stay as Required lines.

## Answer

Convention on [[plan/roadmap/map.md]] and each Epic file. Chapter vs Required for done, no overlap. Wiki portions about this Epic gate this Epic.
