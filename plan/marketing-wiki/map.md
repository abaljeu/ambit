# Marketing wiki

Labels: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A GitLab-level browsable wiki that describes uses of Gambol (who it is for, what jobs it does, example situations). Not a campaign: no ads, no launch plan, no funnel.

## Notes

- Charted from [[plan/roadmap/map.md]] after [[plan/roadmap/issues/02-inventory-live-projects-and-roadmap-remainder.md]].
- “GitLab-level browsable” means a public-quality page tree you can walk (use pages, navigation), comparable to GitLab’s product/use documentation, not a hosted campaign site.
- Sister Projects: [[plan/end-user-wiki/map.md]] (describe the software / how to operate it), [[plan/architecture/map.md]] (how it is coded and run).

## Decisions so far

- Uses, not a campaign.
- Browsable at GitLab documentation quality, not a one-page pitch.

## Not yet specified

- Home: a `doc/` subtree, a GitLab wiki/Pages tree, in-app Graph content, or another browse tree.
- First use pages besides documents from any connected device (that use is a child ticket).
- Boundary vs End-user wiki (how-to vs why/where you would use it).

## Out of scope

- A marketing campaign, ads, SEO program, or launch calendar.
- Operator how-to (End-user wiki) and coding/runtime internals (Architecture).
- Implementing product features.
