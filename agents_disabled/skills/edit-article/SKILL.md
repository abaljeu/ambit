1. Divide the article into sections based on its headings. Think about the main points you want to make during those sections.

Consider that information is a directed acyclic graph, and that pieces of information can depend on other pieces of information. Make sure that the order of the sections and their contents respects these dependencies.

Confirm the sections with the user.
Done: the user has confirmed the section list and dependency order.

2. For each section:

2a. Rewrite the section to improve clarity, coherence, and flow. Use maximum 240 characters per paragraph.
Done: the section is rewritten in the article file and every paragraph is at most 240 characters.

Done (step 2): every confirmed section has completed 2a.
