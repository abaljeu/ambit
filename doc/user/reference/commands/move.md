# Move

Category: Reference

See Also:

[Commands](doc/user/reference/commands.md)

These commands rearrange nodes, and the selection stays on them.

## Move Up

Keys: Alt+Up arrow, Ctrl+Up arrow, and Ctrl+`,`. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Moves the selected nodes one place up. At the start of the list, they can move into the previous open sibling or out beside the parent.

## Move Down

Keys: Alt+Down arrow, Ctrl+Down arrow, and Ctrl+`o`. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Moves the selected nodes one place down. At the end of the list, they can move into the next open sibling or out beside the parent.

## Move Selection to Start

Keys: Alt+Page Up. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Moves the selected nodes to the first place under their parent.

## Move Selection to End

Keys: Alt+Page Down. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Moves the selected nodes to the last place under their parent.

## Move Selection to Top of View

Keys: Alt+Home. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Moves the selected nodes to the first place under the view root.

## Move Selection to End of View

Keys: Alt+End. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Moves the selected nodes to the last place under the view root.

## Indent

Keys: Tab. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Makes the selected nodes children of the sibling just before them. At the first sibling, Indent does nothing.

## Outdent

Keys: Shift+Tab. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Makes the selected nodes siblings of their parent. When that parent is the view root, the view moves up so the nodes stay visible.

## Move Selected

Keys: `m` and Ctrl+M. While you are selecting or editing.

What it does: Opens a search. The selected nodes move to the place after the node you pick.
